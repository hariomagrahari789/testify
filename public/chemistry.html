Atomic Structure

Fundamental Concepts

Atoms are the basic building blocks of matter. They consist of three main subatomic particles:

  • Protons: Positively charged particles in the nucleus
  • Neutrons: Neutral particles in the nucleus
  • Electrons: Negatively charged particles orbiting the nucleus

Atomic Number (Z)

Number of protons in an atom's nucleus. Determines the element.

Mass Number (A)

Sum of protons and neutrons in the nucleus.

Isotopes

Atoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons.

Electron Configuration

Electrons are arranged in energy levels (shells) around the nucleus. Each shell has subshells (s, p, d, f) with specific orbitals.

Aufbau Principle: Electrons fill orbitals starting with the lowest energy level
Pauli Exclusion Principle: Each orbital holds max 2 electrons with opposite spins
Hund's Rule: Electrons fill orbitals singly before pairing

Quantum Mechanical Model

Describes electrons as both particles and waves. Key concepts:

  • Electrons exist in orbitals (probability clouds)
  • Each electron has a unique set of quantum numbers
  • Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle: Impossible to know position and momentum simultaneously
